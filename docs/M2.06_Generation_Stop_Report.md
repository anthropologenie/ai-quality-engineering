## 4. Why the sprint stopped

**Implementation stopped because real model-backed generation could not be introduced consistently under the frozen Generation Contract v1.0.0 without changing repository authority.**

The conflict was not incidental to one clause. It appeared independently across four of the contract's fourteen normative guarantees (`docs/GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §16), and the contract carried no clause under which an implementing sprint could resolve any of them.

**These guarantees are not recorded here as defects.** Each was correct, coherent, and true of the component it described — the Milestone 1A deterministic quotation Generator. The STOP occurred because the capability M2-06 was to introduce required semantics the frozen contract did not authorize, which is a different thing from the contract being wrong.

### 4.1 G-14 — the I/O boundary

**Repository fact.** `docs/GENERATION_CONTRACT.md:457` — *"**G-14 — Observational purity.** Generation SHALL perform no filesystem I/O, **no network I/O**, and no mutation of the `RetrievalResult` it consumes."*

**The conflict.** A real external provider-backed generator requires a sanctioned provider interaction. That interaction is network I/O. The guarantee prohibits it without qualification, and admits no scoped exception.

### 4.2 G-9 — determinism

**Repository fact.** `docs/GENERATION_CONTRACT.md:447` — *"**G-9 — Determinism.** Repeated execution over an identical query and an identical corpus state SHALL produce field-for-field equal results, and SHALL produce byte-identical serialized output under §13.2."* §12 (`:343`) records that this is achieved *"by **excluding the values that would break it**"* rather than by testing for it, and §17 invariant 13 restates it.

**The conflict.** Externally generated model output is exactly such a value. The byte-identical determinism guarantee **could not honestly be applied** to it. Asserting the guarantee while calling a model would have made the contract state something the implementation could not support.

### 4.3 G-7 — support by construction

**Repository fact.** `docs/GENERATION_CONTRACT.md:443` — *"**G-7 — Support by construction.** Every `GeneratedStatement.text` SHALL be supported by repository evidence **by construction** — the text SHALL be derivable from its `supporting_evidence` spans by **verbatim quotation and deterministic template assembly alone**."* §8.1 correspondingly requires `answer_text` on the Answer path to contain *"no content not present in"* the statements.

**The conflict.** Synthesized model answer text is neither verbatim quotation nor deterministic template assembly. The guarantee was incompatible with the answer text a real generator produces.

### 4.4 G-13 / §18 — the runtime dependency boundary

**Repository facts.** `docs/GENERATION_CONTRACT.md:455` — *"**G-13** … The Generator SHALL depend at runtime on **the query and a `RetrievalResult` only**."* §18 (`:507`, `:511`) restates the permitted set as *"the request `query`, and a `RetrievalResult` produced by the Retriever."*

**The conflict, in two parts.**

*First*, the M2-12 `Prompt` (`sample_rag/context_builder.py:171–173`: `query`, `context`, `chunk_ids`) is not a `RetrievalResult`. The frozen boundary did not authorize the transition to it. §6.2 anticipated the change in narrative terms — recording the `docs/architecture.md` §5 signature as *"the Milestone 2 target, reached when a Context Builder exists"* — but §18 as ratified carries no corresponding permission, and a narrative anticipation in a rejected-options table is not an authorization.

*Second*, and independently: `SupportingEvidence` (§8.3, `:252`) requires `chunk_id`, `document_id`, `character_start`, `character_end` and `text`. `Prompt.chunk_ids` carries only chunk identity. **The Generator could not simply reach back into retrieval or corpus state to obtain the missing evidence metadata** — G-13 and §18 bar precisely that. The two halves of the conflict therefore closed on each other: the Prompt was not an authorized input, and even had it been, it did not carry what the artifact contract required.

### 4.5 The conflict was enforced executably, not only in prose

The v1.0.0 guarantees were frozen by committed specifications, so the conflict was not resolvable by reinterpretation:

- `tests/test_generator.py:695–696` — an AST **allowlist**: `imported_roots(module) <= {"json", "dataclasses", "sample_rag"}` and `imported_modules(module) <= {"json", "dataclasses", "sample_rag.retriever"}`. A provider client import fails it; **so does importing `sample_rag.context_builder` to obtain `Prompt`**.
- `tests/test_generator.py:756` — bars the `urllib` and `socket` roots.
- Further specifications in the same file freeze G-7, G-9 and G-13/G-14 behaviourally.

Proceeding would have required rewriting the specifications that are the executable form of four frozen guarantees. **The sprint did not modify them.**

### 4.6 A second, independent conflict — M2-06's own recorded scope

**Repository fact.** `docs/DEFERRED_ITEMS_REGISTER.md:172` (at `0b781d9`) described **M2-06** as *"Replaces the quotation Generator **behind the frozen `generate(query, retrieval)` signature**."*

**Repository fact.** The **M2-12** discharge row in the same file recorded the Generator input transition as *"**handed to M2-06**"*.

**The conflict.** One authority described M2-06 as retaining the v1.0.0 signature; the other described M2-06 as owning the transition away from it. **Both could not hold.** This was a governance ambiguity between committed authorities, not an engineering question, and the sprint had no basis in repository evidence for preferring either reading.

### 4.7 Dependency criterion

**Repository fact.** `docs/DEFERRED_ITEMS_REGISTER.md` §4 records criterion **A-5** (*"Zero imports of any embedding, vector-store, or LLM-evaluation library"*) as having exactly two exceptions — `sentence-transformers` at Sprint M2.01A and `faiss-cpu` at Sprint M2.01B — and states that *"the **LLM SDK** portion (**M2-06**) … [is] untouched and still hold[s]"*.

**The observation.** Introducing a provider dependency would have lapsed the criterion a third time. **No dependency was added**, and the sprint did not select one.

## 5. U-1, U-2 and U-3 at the time of the STOP

The three forward questions M2-12 handed to M2-06 were **all unresolved when the sprint stopped**, and this section records that status as it stood.

### U-1 — Generator input transition

*What Generator input contract should connect the M2-12 `Prompt` artifact to real generation?*

**UNRESOLVED at STOP.** §4.4 and §4.6 above record why: the frozen boundary did not authorize a `Prompt` input, and two committed authorities disagreed on whether M2-06 owned the change at all. The sprint reached no decision, and recorded none.

### U-2 — Prompt provenance

*Is the M2-12 three-field `Prompt` sufficient to construct a conforming `SupportingEvidence`, or is additional minimal provenance required?*

**UNRESOLVED at STOP.** The sprint established the shortfall as a repository fact — §8.3 requires `document_id` and both document-frame offsets, `chunk_ids` carries neither, and G-13 barred fetching them — but **selected no provenance shape, authorized no field, and modified no artifact.** `Prompt` remained `query`, `context`, `chunk_ids`.

### U-3 — Runtime reachability

*How, if at all, should the runtime become reachable from retrieval and context assembly through to generation?*

**UNRESOLVED at STOP.** `evaluation/altm_rules.py` `REACHABLE_STAGES` was not widened. **No orchestration layer was authorized or created**, no runtime adapter or pipeline coordinator was introduced, and `scripts/cli.py` was not modified.
