## 3. Metric semantics, derived

### 3.1 The two definitions, quoted

`docs/AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 3 — tool-independent, and the source RO-17 Decision 1 names:

| Metric | Definition, as written |
|---|---|
| **Context Precision** | *"Of everything retrieved, how much was actually relevant?"* |
| **Context Recall** | *"Of everything relevant that exists in the source, how much did retrieval actually find?"* |

`docs/altm.md` §6 places both at the **Retrieve** stage and adds the sentence that fixes Context Precision's frame:

> Context Precision — *"Measures noise in retrieved evidence — **has nothing to do with what is eventually said**"*
> Context Recall — *"Measures whether relevant evidence was found at all — also independent of generation"*

### 3.2 The derivation

| | Context Precision | Context Recall |
|---|---|---|
| **Metric unit** | one **retrieved passage** | one **reference claim** |
| **Reference unit** | none — the **question** | the golden **`expected_answer`**, decomposed into claims |
| **Relevance semantics** | semantic: does this passage bear on the question? | semantic: is this claim supported by the retrieved context? |
| **Ordering** | **does not matter** | does not matter |
| **LLM judge required** | **yes** | **yes** |
| **Aggregation** | macro and micro, both | macro and micro, both |

**Why the metric unit is a passage, not an identifier.** The reference document's own Context Precision example enumerates retrieved chunks and labels each relevant or irrelevant — *"CrewAI experience, RCA pipeline (relevant) + a Bosch internship bullet (irrelevant)"*. The unit being counted is the retrieved item and the property being counted is its relevance, which is a property of its **content**.

**Why the Context Recall reference is `expected_answer` and not `expected_chunk`.** Recall over expected chunk identifiers is precisely `|E ∩ O| / |E|` — which the repository already implements, already names, and already forbids reporting under this name. `docs/P3.3.3_Retrieval_Metrics_Report.md` §3:

> *"Chunk Precision@K and Chunk Recall@K are not those metrics. They are deterministic set-arithmetic measurements over a committed evaluation. They do not approximate Ragas metrics, are not a proxy for them, and must not be reported under their names."*

RO-17 Decision 1 restates the bar for this sprint — *"a simplified set-overlap calculation is NOT authorized as a substitute for the metric's meaning"*. The reference document's Context Recall example makes the difference concrete: *"Resume mentions 'CrewAI' twice; retrieval surfaces only one occurrence."* The thing recovered or missed is **information**, and a corpus chunk stating the same fact at a different id satisfies the metric while failing identifier overlap. **`expected_chunk` is therefore not read by the metric engine at all**, which `test_m207_context_recall_is_not_chunk_identifier_overlap` asserts structurally.

**Why ordering does not participate.** No repository authority ranks, discounts or position-weights either metric. *"Of everything retrieved, how much was actually relevant"* is a proportion over a set, and *"noise in retrieved evidence"* is order-free. A rank-weighted variant would be a different metric imported from outside the repository's own definitions, so **`context_precision` is invariant under permutation of the retrieved passages** — asserted by `test_m207_context_precision_carries_rank_without_depending_on_it`, not assumed. Rank is still carried into the evidence, because diagnosis wants it even though the metric does not.

**Why a judge is genuinely required.** Both propositions are semantic — whether a passage bears on a question, and whether a claim is supported by text — and no set operation over identifiers can decide either. **This is established from the definitions, not inherited from the fact that the abandoned Ragas path used a judge.** RO-17 Decision 6 left the question open for this sprint; the answer is yes, for both metrics.

**Why both aggregations are published.** `docs/P3.3.3_…` §3 records the repository's position, and the reasoning is unchanged by the metric's name:

> *"No repository authority selects between them, and they answer different questions: macro weights every question equally, micro weights every chunk equally. Selecting one here would make a methodological choice on Sprint P3.3.4's behalf while presenting it as a measurement."*

### 3.3 The three judged propositions

**Fixed before the metric is computed, not discovered from judge output.** Each is a closed question with a single-token answer; the metric is an aggregation over those answers, never a score the judge produces directly.

| # | Proposition | Inputs | Vocabulary |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | *Does this passage contain information relevant to answering the question?* | question, one retrieved passage | `RELEVANT` / `NOT_RELEVANT` |
| 2 | *What individual factual claims does this reference answer make?* | question, `expected_answer` | one claim per line |
| 3 | *Is this claim supported by the retrieved context?* | one claim, the retrieved context | `SUPPORTED` / `NOT_SUPPORTED` |

**Proposition 1 is judged against the question alone.** The reference answer is deliberately withheld: `docs/altm.md` §6 records that Context Precision *"has nothing to do with what is eventually said"*, and showing the judge the expected answer would convert *relevant to the question* into *supports the expected answer* — Context Recall's proposition under Context Precision's name, drifting back toward the identifier overlap RO-17 bars.

**Proposition 2 never sees retrieval output, and structurally cannot.** `decomposition_prompt(question, reference_answer)` has no parameter through which retrieved context could arrive. This is the non-circularity RO-17 Decision 6 requires — *"the reference is the committed golden ground truth — never the system's own retrieval output"* — expressed as a signature rather than as a promise about a call site, and asserted twice by `test_m207_the_recall_reference_never_sees_retrieval_output`.

**Why proposition 2 is judged rather than split deterministically.** A sentence splitter is deterministic and is not faithful to this dataset: the golden reference *"Maruti Suzuki, Betts Group, and Red Earth."* is one sentence and three recoverable facts, and scoring it as a single unit would make **partial recovery unrepresentable** — the exact phenomenon Context Recall exists to detect. The deterministic option was rejected for being unfaithful, not for being inconvenient. **The cost is stated rather than hidden: the recall denominator is not deterministic**, and the per-case evidence records the claims a run actually used.

### 3.4 The aggregation rule

```text
per case    context_precision = relevant retrieved passages / retrieved passages
            context_recall    = supported reference claims  / reference claims

macro       mean of the unrounded per-case ratios, rounded once at publication
micro       Σ numerators / Σ denominators
```

Rounded to **4 places** — `evaluation/retrieval_metrics.py`'s `PRECISION`, so these figures are directly comparable with the `chunk_` metrics they are deliberately not. `math.fsum` is used for macro accumulation so reordering the dataset cannot move the last digit.

**Documented zero denominators**, both reachable and neither an error:

| Denominator | Behaviour |
|---|---|
| retrieved passages = 0 | `context_precision = 0.0`, **no judge call made** — the convention `evaluation/retrieval_metrics.py` already documents for `chunk_precision_at_k` |
| retrieval empty (recall) | `context_recall = 0.0`, `claims_total = 0`, `unmeasured_reason` recorded, **no judge call made** |
| cases = 0 | all aggregates `0.0` — the report stays total |

### 4.1 The two abstention entries, and why the dataset is partitioned rather than edited

Both `Abstain` reference answers open with an **absence** claim:

> *"The resume does not state any Kubernetes experience. The DevOps and platform engineering skills it lists are …"*
> *"The resume does not state compensation for any role. It states that he was QA Lead / Test Module Lead at Happiest Minds Technologies …"*

Context Recall's denominator is, in the repository's own words, *"everything relevant that **exists** in the source"*. A claim about what the source does **not** contain is not information retrieval could recover, and the Retrieve stage cannot establish absence in any case — five retrieved passages not containing a fact is not the corpus not containing it. Such a claim is therefore **outside the metric's domain**, not a retrieval failure.

**The response is a documented domain statement, not a dataset edit and not an exclusion.** Context Recall is reported **twice**: over the 20 `Answer` entries (the domain-faithful figure) and over all 22 (published beside it). A partition that only ever appeared as a filtered number would be indistinguishable from a partition chosen for its number, so both are printed and the difference is visible. **Context Precision is not partitioned** — whether a retrieved passage is relevant to a question is well defined for an abstention question too, and both abstention entries carry expected evidence chunks of their own.

## 5. Provider-boundary gate — RO-17 Decision 4

**The condition is satisfied, and the finding is that nothing had to change.**

RO-17 Decision 4 authorizes `sample_rag/deepseek.py` as M2-07's judge boundary *"if and only if"* the native implementation can use it **without** modifying its provider architecture, changing its credential model, introducing another provider integration or SDK, introducing a model router, introducing arbitrary HTTP access, or otherwise widening it.

| Requirement | Finding |
|---|---|
| Judge reachable through the existing boundary as it stands | **Yes.** `DeepSeekClient.complete(messages) -> str` is already the whole of what a judge needs |
| `sample_rag/deepseek.py` modified | **No** — byte-identical, SHA-256 `281192b3…`, asserted by `test_m207_the_authorized_provider_boundary_was_not_widened` |
| Second provider client created | **No.** `test_m207_no_second_provider_client_was_created` asserts `sample_rag/deepseek.py` is still the only module in `sample_rag/`, `scripts/` or `evaluation/` holding a network primitive |
| Provider SDK / model router / arbitrary HTTP added | **No** |
| Credential architecture changed | **No.** `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` is still read inside `complete` alone; the evaluation path never reads, receives, prints or reports it |
| Live provider call in deterministic pytest | **No** — see §7 |

**The whole of the reuse is one adapter in `scripts/evaluate_context.py`:**

```python
def judge(prompt: str) -> str:
    return client.complete([{"role": "user", "content": prompt}])
```

The metric engine holds **no provider concept at all** — it takes a `judge(prompt) -> str` callable — so the provider's message shape is adapted at this one seam and nowhere else. No client is created, modified, subclassed or wrapped in a second architecture.

**RO-16 Decision 3 is not relied on.** Its condition was expressly scoped to *"the selected Ragas API"*; with Ragas off the path that condition has no subject. The authority used here is **RO-17 Decision 4**, on its own terms.

**Unchanged, and asserted so:** `sample_rag/fusion.py`, `sample_rag/embedding.py`, `sample_rag/vector_index.py`, `sample_rag/retriever.py`, `sample_rag/context_builder.py`, `sample_rag/model_generator.py`, `sample_rag/generator.py`, retriever behaviour, `ContextBuilder` behaviour, `top_k`, the RRF constants, ranking thresholds, chunking and prompts. `test_m207_the_evaluation_entry_point_measures_retrieval_without_changing_it` fails if the orchestrator ever declares a `top_k`, `rrf`, `threshold`, `rerank`, `rewrite`, `expand`, `weight`, `boost`, `truncat`, `budget`, `retry` or `fallback` name.

**M2-07 is MEASURE, not IMPROVE.** No retrieval change was made, and none is proposed here.

### 6.3 What was deliberately not built

**No independent validator module.** `evaluation/retrieval_metrics_validator.py` and `evaluation/retrieval_diagnosis_validator.py` establish the precedent, and it was considered and declined: the engine's aggregation is two ratios and a mean, and RO-17 asks for the *smallest* native implementation. The cross-check the precedent exists for is kept — `test_m207_aggregation_is_re_derivable_over_exact_rationals` re-derives the published figures in `Fraction` — inside the specification rather than as a second module. **Recorded as an implementation finding rather than a silent omission.**

**No sampling, subset or limit argument on the entry point.** The dataset is evaluated whole on every run, so a reported figure is never a figure over cases someone chose.

**No file is written by the evaluation path.** `reports/baseline/` and `reports/regressions/` are **M3-03** scaffolds and are not populated; the M2-18 trace is not written, not imported and not extended.

## 8. Credential and payload discipline

| | |
|---|---|
| Credential read by the evaluation path | **Never.** `DEEPSEEK_API_KEY` is read inside `sample_rag/deepseek.py`'s `complete` alone; `scripts/evaluate_context.py` never reads, receives, prints, logs or reports it — not even as a boolean |
| Raw judge **request** payload persisted or logged | **No.** Prompts are constructed, sent and discarded; nothing in the metric report holds one, asserted by `test_m207_the_report_carries_the_evidence_that_explains_each_score` |
| Raw judge **response** payload persisted or logged | **No.** Only the **parsed verdict** and the **parsed claim text** survive parsing |
| Provider metadata persisted | Only the **model identifier**, which `DeepSeekClient.model` already documents as *"safe to record as sprint evidence"* |
| Credential in an exception message | **Structurally impossible.** `ContextMetricsError` reports the *proposition* being judged — case id, chunk id, claim index — and never quotes the judge's answer; `test_m207_a_failed_judgement_never_quotes_what_the_judge_returned` asserts it |
| Files written by the evaluation path | **None.** Output goes to stdout |

**On recording parsed claim text.** A claim is **derived evaluation evidence**, not a raw payload: it is the metric's denominator, it is derived solely from the committed golden `expected_answer` (the decomposition call receives no other content), and a recall score cannot be explained without it — *which* information was missed is the diagnostic question the metric exists to answer. It carries no credential, no header and no provider metadata. **The raw response document that carried it is not retained.** This sprint did not need any raw provider payload to compute or explain either metric, so none is persisted and no STOP on that ground arose.

## 9. Instrument validation — the relevance-proposition correction

**A first live run produced a Context Precision of `0.2727`. That figure is an instrument artifact and is not reported as a result.**

**How it was caught.** The per-case evidence was internally contradictory. `meta_qa_resume_f005_lexical_01` scored **`context_precision` 0/5 alongside `context_recall` 3/3** — every reference claim judged supported by a context in which no passage was judged relevant. Those two cannot both be true, so one of the two propositions was not being answered as written.

**What the judge was actually answering.** Asked for its reasoning on the rank-1 passage of that case, the judge returned:

> *"The passage mentions leading a team of 5 … but it specifies this was for 'QA delivery' and does not state the number of engineers Karthik led specifically for the dealer service analytics platform. The question asks for the count of engineers, while the passage refers to a 'cross-functional team,' which may include non-engineers, so the information is not directly sufficient to answer the question."*
> — verdict `NOT_RELEVANT`, returned three times out of three

The judge was applying a **sufficiency** standard: *does this passage settle the question exactly and on its own?* The repository's definition asks a different question. `docs/AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 3 asks *"of everything retrieved, how much was actually **relevant**"* and illustrates it by topic — *"CrewAI experience, RCA pipeline (relevant) + a Bosch internship bullet (irrelevant)"* — and `docs/altm.md` §6 names the quantity *"noise in retrieved evidence"*. **Noise is content that does not bear on the question, not content that fails to settle it.**

**The repository states the positive/negative distinction directly, and states it as topical.** `docs/roadmap.md` §8: *"Retrieval's **only** failure mode is pulling in real-but-irrelevant material (noise)."* `docs/glossary.md`: Context Precision is *"how much was actually relevant **versus noise**."* `docs/altm.md` §4: the Retrieve failure is *"irrelevant chunks crowd out relevant ones"*, and `docs/architecture.md` §9 gives the stage's quality attributes as *"Relevance, noise minimization"*. `docs/learning-log.md` records that defining Context Precision *"took three attempts"* and that the settled reading is *"precision **scoped to retrieval**"*, the second attempt having been rejected as *"a category error, since retrieval can only pull in **real-but-irrelevant** material"*. **Under every one of these, the negative class is material that does not bear on the question — never material that bears on it without settling it.**

**The repository also binds the metric to that mechanism in executable form.** `evaluation/altm_rules.py` `ALTM-RETRIEVE-4` carries `"metric": "Context Precision"` with symptom *"Low precision on a known-answerable query"* and investigation *"Check for **near-duplicate or loosely-related** chunks crowding results"* — near-duplication and loose relatedness, which are topical properties.

**A sufficiency standard would also relocate the metric to two other layers the repository keeps separate.** Whether one chunk carries a complete semantic unit is **Chunk Integrity** — a **Layer 2 / Index** metric (*"Did chunk boundaries preserve a complete semantic/business unit?"*), evaluated deterministically without a judge. Whether something *"directly, **completely**, and only address[es] what was actually asked"* is **Answer Relevancy**, a **Layer 5 / Final Answer** metric. Reading completeness into Context Precision would make a **Retrieve**-stage metric vary with chunking granularity and answer completeness, collapsing exactly the boundary `docs/learning-log.md` calls *"the single most load-bearing distinction in the entire documentation set."*

**The correction.** `relevance_prompt` now states the standard explicitly — relevance is subject matter, not sufficiency; a passage that answers only partly, only approximately, or only together with other passages is `RELEVANT`; a passage is `NOT_RELEVANT` only if it is about a different subject or carries no information bearing on the question.

**How it was validated, and how it was not.** Against a **labelled probe in both directions**, so a proposition that simply answered `RELEVANT` to everything would fail as loudly as the original failed the positives. **It was not tuned against the aggregate score**, and the aggregate was not consulted while choosing the wording.

| Probe case | Label | Original | Corrected |
|---|---|---|---|
| *"Led QA delivery … managing a cross-functional team of 5"* ← *how many engineers led?* | RELEVANT | ❌ `NOT_RELEVANT` | ✅ |
| *"Led a cross-functional QA team of 5, owning test strategy …"* ← same question | RELEVANT | ❌ `NOT_RELEVANT` | ✅ |

**On the two figures, stated precisely.** The eleven rows above are the **common scored set**, and both columns are scored over it: **9/11 → 11/11**. A twelfth case was put to the original proposition and is **not** in that set — the bare heading *"Maruti Suzuki India — Service Performance & Customer Experience Analytics Platform"* against *"how many engineers …"* — so the original's raw figure over all twelve was **9/12**. It was **dropped from scoring rather than graded**: whether a heading naming the question's subject but carrying no figure is relevant is genuinely arguable, and grading an instrument against a contestable label would be grading it against a preference.

**The dropped case is one the original proposition failed**, so removing it *raises* the original's ratio (9/12 → 9/11) rather than lowering it. The comparison is therefore conservative toward the original, and the two cases the correction actually fixed are both inside the common set. **An earlier revision of this section reported the pair as `9/12 → 11/11`, which compared different denominators**; it is corrected here, and the 9/12 figure is retained above rather than deleted because it is what the original proposition actually scored.

**The attribution proposition was probed the same way and needed no change** — 6/6 in both directions, including `NOT_SUPPORTED` for *"Karthik led a team of 50"* and *"Karthik reduced ETL test execution time by 40%"* against a context supporting neither.

`test_m207_the_relevance_proposition_states_relevance_not_sufficiency` pins the corrected standard so the regression is detectable.

## 13. Limitations and findings

**M2.07-F-1 — neither metric is deterministic, and the recall denominator is the wider source of it.** A live model judges every verdict, and the reference decomposition is a judge product too: the two runs performed during this sprint decomposed the same 22 committed reference answers into **143** and **133** claims. Precision's denominator is fixed by retrieval and only its numerator varies; recall varies in **both**. **Consequence:** a re-run reproduces the figures approximately, not exactly, and no determinism is claimed for either. **The engine itself is deterministic given a deterministic judge**, which `test_m207_repeated_execution_over_a_fixed_judge_is_identical` establishes, so the variability is attributable to the provider rather than to the measurement.

**M2.07-F-2 — the judged proposition is part of the instrument, and a faithful-sounding one was not faithful.** §9 records a proposition that read correctly, passed every deterministic specification, and measured the wrong thing — and was caught only because the per-case evidence was internally contradictory. **The specifications cannot catch this class of defect**, because they supply the judge's answers. **Consequence:** any future change to a prompt in `evaluation/context_metrics.py` is a change to the metric's meaning and requires re-validation against a labelled probe, not merely a green suite. This is the strongest argument for the per-case evidence RO-17 Decision 6 requires: the score alone would have looked plausible.

**M2.07-F-3 — no threshold, target or acceptance criterion exists for either metric anywhere in the repository.** Searched and confirmed absent. **Consequence:** the figures in §10 are evidence and cannot be read as a pass or a failure. Establishing a target is a Repository Owner decision, not an implementing sprint's.

**M2.07-F-4 — Context Recall's domain excludes absence claims, and the dataset contains two entries that assert them.** §4.1 records the derivation and publishes both partitions. **Consequence:** the Answer-only figure is the domain-faithful one; the all-entries figure is published beside it so the partition is inspectable. **No golden entry was modified or removed.**

**M2.07-F-5 — a single unreadable verdict aborts a whole run.** `parse_verdict` raises rather than defaulting, and `sample_rag/deepseek.py` performs no retry, so one malformed judge answer in ~265 calls ends the evaluation with no score. **This is deliberate** — a default verdict would fabricate a measurement, and a fabricated zero is indistinguishable in a report from a judged one — but it is a real operational fragility and is recorded as such rather than solved speculatively. **Non-blocking.** It did not occur in either run performed.

**M2.07-F-6 — no independent validator module was created**, against the precedent of `evaluation/retrieval_metrics_validator.py` and `evaluation/retrieval_diagnosis_validator.py`. The engine's aggregation is two ratios and a mean, and RO-17 asks for the smallest native implementation; the cross-check the precedent exists for is kept as `test_m207_aggregation_is_re_derivable_over_exact_rationals`, which re-derives the published figures over `Fraction`. **Recorded as a deliberate departure from precedent rather than an omission.**

**M2.07-F-7 — the corpus contains three versions of one source document and 52.5% duplicated chunk text.** §11 records the evidence and the retrieval consequence. **This is an observation, not a repair**: it is an Index/Knowledge-stage property, and acting on it is **M2-17** (chunking) or **M2-15** (retrieval quality), neither of which RO-17 authorizes. **Observability is not repair.**

**No authority conflict was encountered.** No frozen contract required changing, no dependency was required, no embedding or vector-store capability was needed, the existing provider boundary was sufficient unmodified, no raw provider payload was needed to compute or explain either metric, and no governance document required editing.
