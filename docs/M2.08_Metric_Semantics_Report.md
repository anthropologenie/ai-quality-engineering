## 3. The Generation Contract findings that govern this sprint

RO-18 Decision 8 names `docs/GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` as a metric-defining authority. Four of its findings are load-bearing here and are recorded before the metric definitions, because three of the four metrics are unsound without them.

### 3.1 §24.3 — the three-way distinction

Quoted in full, because it is the single most important constraint on this sprint:

| | What it is | Established by |
|---|---|---|
| **Structural evidence provenance** | The evidence chain `retrieved chunk → ContextBuilder → Prompt context + provenance → Generator → SupportingEvidence`, checkable from the artifact | This contract |
| **Model answer synthesis** | That `answer_text` is generated, not quoted | This contract |
| **Faithfulness / Groundedness** | Whether the synthesized answer is actually supported by its evidence | **Later evaluation work — neither established nor claimed here.** |

§25.1 point 5 restates it and adds the sentence that closes the inference: **"That a synthesized `answer_text` is permitted is not a statement that it is faithful."**

**Consequence for M2-08, stated as the sprint's first rule.** The contract guarantees that every `SupportingEvidence` the artifact carries *came from* the assembled `Prompt` provenance. It guarantees **nothing** about whether the claim that evidence is attached to is entailed by it. **The system's own attribution is therefore not admissible as the reference for any M2-08 metric** — using it would convert a structural guarantee into the empirical property M2-08 exists to measure, which is exactly the conflation §24.3 was written to prevent. This is applied in §8 (circularity) and enforced in the judge propositions of §9.

### 3.2 §8.3 `SupportingEvidence` — and what it is and is not

`SupportingEvidence` carries `chunk_id`, `document_id`, `character_start`, `character_end`, `text`, with `text == document_text[character_start:character_end]` (**G-11**) and `chunk_id` resolving to a real corpus chunk (**G-5** as read under §25.2, **G-6**). §7.1 records what it is: *"the runtime realization of 'a supporting fact': a citable span with a document-frame location."*

**It is a citable span, and it is a system-selected one.** Both halves matter. The *citable span* half is why the repository's Groundedness definition — *"traced to a specific, citable piece of evidence"* — has a runtime referent at all. The *system-selected* half is why that referent must be re-established empirically by the evaluator rather than read off the artifact.

**How M2-08 uses it.** As **diagnostic evidence** — recording what the system claimed to be citing beside what the judge found — and **never as the metric's reference**. RO-18 Decision 8 requires M2-08 to *"preserve enough evidence to explain a metric result"*; this is one of the things worth preserving. It does not enter any numerator or denominator.

### 3.3 G-7 and the guarantees adjacent to it

**G-7 under v1.0.0** required `answer_text` to be *"derivable from its `supporting_evidence` spans by verbatim quotation and deterministic template assembly alone"*, and stated outright: *"No semantic entailment, claim verification, hallucination detection, faithfulness evaluation or groundedness evaluation is introduced by this contract; those are Milestone 2."*

**G-7 under v2.0.0** (§24.3) is *narrowed to provenance*: `answer_text` **MAY be synthesized**; `SupportingEvidence` **SHALL remain grounded in the assembled `Prompt` context and its provenance, and in nothing else.**

The G-7-adjacent guarantees that bear on the metric definitions:

| Guarantee | Status under v2.0.0 | Why M2-08 depends on it |
|---|---|---|
| **G-4** — every statement carries ≥1 `SupportingEvidence` | Unchanged | Makes the claim a first-class artifact element; §14 records that this exists *because* Groundedness is defined per claim |
| **G-5 / G-6** — evidence identity resolves to the committed corpus | Unchanged (G-5 read through §25.2 via `Prompt.provenance`) | Lets a judged result be traced back to a chunk and a document for diagnosis |
| **G-7** (surviving half) | Narrowed to provenance | Fixes the **assembled `Prompt` context** as the only evidence frame the generator may draw from — and therefore the only frame the Infer-stage metrics may judge against (§6.1) |
| **G-8** — `statements` empty **iff** `outcome == "Abstain"` | Unchanged | Fixes the zero-claim case exactly, which is what makes the abstention domain statement in §7.4 precise rather than conventional |
| **G-9** — split into structural determinism / model-output reproducibility | Split | The metric is measured over a **non-reproducible** `answer_text`; no metric value may be presented as deterministic |
| **G-3** — `answer_text` non-empty on every path | Unchanged | There is always something to evaluate, including on Abstain |
| **G-14** — one sanctioned provider interaction | Narrowed | The generation-side boundary; the M2-08 judge seam is separately and narrowly authorized by RO-18 Decision 4 |

**§25.1 point 2 is decisive for the evaluation unit** (§6.2): §8.1's clause that `answer_text` is *"assembled from the `statements` … and contains no content not present in them"* is *"the descriptive restatement of G-7 … and it does not survive the transition of the guarantee it restates."* **Under v2.0.0 the delivered answer may therefore contain content that appears in no `GeneratedStatement`.**

### 3.4 What the contract already excludes

§21 excludes *"Every Layer 3/4 metric: Faithfulness, Groundedness, Hallucination Rate, Answer Relevancy"* and *"Semantic entailment, claim verification, hallucination detection."* §15 records why `faithfulness`, `groundedness` and `hallucination_rate` are not artifact fields: *"A runtime artifact carrying its own quality scores would also make the system under test grade itself — the coupling `docs/architecture.md` §6 separates `sample_rag/` from `evaluation/` to prevent."*

**Both exclusions are preserved by this report.** M2-08 lives in `evaluation/`, reads the artifact, and adds no field to it.

## 5. What is evaluated, and against what

Two structural questions must be settled before any metric can be stated, because all four depend on them.

### 5.1 The evidence frame is the assembled `Prompt` context

The authorities use two wordings — *"the retrieved context"* (`AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md`, `glossary.md`, `roadmap.md` §5) and *"the assembled prompt"* (`altm.md` §4 Infer, `ALTM-INFER-1`). **These are not in conflict; the second is the more specific statement of the same thing, and it is the one an Infer-stage metric must use.** Three independent authorities converge on it:

1. `altm.md` §4 **Infer** states the failure as *"Model states something not supported by **the assembled prompt**"*, and `ALTM-INFER-1`'s investigation clause is *"**Check assembled prompt for the claim**; if absent, it's fabricated at Infer."*
2. `altm.md` §4 **Assemble** owns the opposite failure — *"Correct chunks were retrieved but did not survive prompt construction"* — with detection by *"Prompt assembly unit/integration tests — **not an LLM metric**."* Judging Faithfulness against the retrieval result rather than the assembled prompt would charge **Infer** with an **Assemble**-stage failure, collapsing a boundary `altm.md` §6 calls out and §7 orders upstream-first.
3. `GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §24.3 fixes the generator's own evidence frame: `SupportingEvidence` *"SHALL remain grounded in the assembled `Prompt` context and its provenance, **and in nothing else**."*

The `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` wording predates the Assemble stage having a referent — the `Prompt` artifact and `ContextBuilder` are M2-12, and `GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §21 excluded both from v1.0.0. **Nothing is amended by this reading**; it records which of two consistent wordings is operative at the Infer stage.

**Recorded consequence.** The metric is defined over the assembled context. Which passages the assembly contains, and in what order, is not this metric's concern and is not evaluated by it.

### 5.2 The claim set is derived from the delivered `answer_text`, not from `statements[]`

`GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §14 records that the claim is a first-class artifact element *because* Groundedness is defined per claim, and names `statements[].text` *"the unit Groundedness quantifies over."* Under **v1.0.0** that was exact: G-7 required `answer_text` to be derivable from the statements by verbatim quotation, and §8.1 required it to contain *"no content not present in them."*

**Under v2.0.0 that equivalence is gone.** §25.1 point 2: §8.1's clause is *"the descriptive restatement of G-7 … and it does not survive the transition of the guarantee it restates"*; §24.3: `answer_text` *"MAY be synthesized by the model"*. A synthesized answer may therefore assert content that appears in no `GeneratedStatement`.

The metric definitions quantify over ***"everything the model claimed"*** (`AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` Faithfulness), ***"everything claimed"*** (Hallucination Rate) and ***"everything the model claimed"*** (`glossary.md`, both). **What the model claimed is what it delivered.** If the claim set were taken from `statements[]`, content synthesized into `answer_text` outside the statements would be unmeasured — and that is precisely where fabrication would live.

**Therefore:** the evaluation unit for Faithfulness, Groundedness and Hallucination Rate is **one claim of the delivered `answer_text`**, obtained by judged decomposition (§9, proposition **P1**). `statements[]` and their `supporting_evidence` are recorded **beside** the result as diagnostic evidence, per RO-18 Decision 8's explainability requirement, and take no part in any numerator or denominator.

This preserves §14's substance — the claim remains the unit Groundedness quantifies over — while respecting the guarantee transition §14 could not anticipate.

## 6. The four metric definitions

Each answers Phase 2's nine questions (A–I) explicitly.

### 6.1 Faithfulness

| | |
|---|---|
| **A — What is evaluated** | Whether the claims the delivered answer makes are supported / entailed by the assembled context |
| **B — Smallest evaluation unit** | **One claim** of `answer_text` |
| **C — What the judge may see** | The claim, and the **assembled context in full** (all passages together) |
| **D — What is authoritative** | The **assembled `Prompt` context** — §5.1. Not the corpus, not the golden `expected_answer`, not the system's `supporting_evidence` |
| **E — The proposition** | *Is this claim supported by the context taken as a whole?* |
| **F — Positive judgment** | `SUPPORTED` — the claim is entailed by the context. **Entailment reached by combining two or more passages counts as supported**: `glossary.md` — *"even if derived by combining multiple sentences … Can be satisfied by multi-hop reasoning across sources"* |
| **G — Negative judgment** | `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`, `CONTRADICTED` or `NOT_SUPPORTED` — none of the three is faithful. The three are kept distinct because Hallucination Rate distinguishes them (§6.3), not because Faithfulness does |
| **H — Aggregation** | Per case: `supported claims / total claims`. Across cases: **macro and micro, both published** |
| **I — Outside the domain** | Whether the context was itself current or correct (`roadmap.md` §5 Layer 3: *"never whether that context was itself current or correct"*; `altm.md` §4 Infer note); whether retrieval found the right evidence (Retrieve stage); whether the answer addresses the question (Final Answer stage); the `Abstain` outcome, which carries zero claims (§7.4) |

### 6.2 Groundedness

| | |
|---|---|
| **A — What is evaluated** | Whether each claim can be traced to **one specific, citable passage** of the assembled context |
| **B — Smallest evaluation unit** | **One claim** of `answer_text` — the same unit as Faithfulness, deliberately, so the two are comparable claim-for-claim |
| **C — What the judge may see** | The claim, and **exactly one passage**, in isolation. It may not see the other passages, and may not see the system's attribution |
| **D — What is authoritative** | **Each individual passage** of the assembled context, taken one at a time |
| **E — The proposition** | *Is this claim supported by this single passage, on its own?* |
| **F — Positive judgment** | The claim is **grounded** iff **at least one passage on its own** supports it. `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md`: *"traced to a specific, citable piece of evidence"*; `glossary.md`: *"traced to one specific, citable piece of evidence"* |
| **G — Negative judgment** | **No single passage supports the claim.** This includes the case where the claim *is* entailed by the passages jointly: `glossary.md` — *"Stricter — weaker on claims that require combining sources without a single direct citation"* |
| **H — Aggregation** | Per case: `grounded claims / total claims`. Across cases: **macro and micro, both published** |
| **I — Outside the domain** | Everything Faithfulness excludes (I above), plus: **whether the passage the claim traces to is the one the system cited.** Groundedness asks whether a citable piece exists, which is what the definition says; whether the system's own citation was the right one is a separate question no repository authority poses as a metric |

**On "substantive" / "important".** `glossary.md` says *"every **important** claim"* and RO-18 D-8 says *"**substantive** answer claims"*, while `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` says *"every **individual** claim"* and `altm.md` §6 says *"**each** claim"* — no qualifier. This divergence is **reported, not silently reconciled**; see §12 finding **U-2**. The operational reading adopted here is the one that requires no invention: the decomposition proposition (P1) yields **claims** — assertions about the corpus — and everything it yields is in Groundedness's domain. The negative form is the repository's own, from `GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §9.3: text that *"asserts nothing about the corpus"* is not a claim. **A narrower importance filter would require an importance standard the repository does not define, and inventing one is barred by RO-18 D-8.**

### 6.3 Hallucination Rate

Defined independently. **It is not defined as `1 − Faithfulness`**, and the repository forbids that identity outright: *"Near-complement of Faithfulness in the simple case; **not guaranteed to sum to 100% once partial/ambiguous claims exist**"* (`altm.md` §6; the same sentence in `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 4 and `glossary.md`).

| | |
|---|---|
| **A — What is evaluated** | How much of what the answer claimed was **fabricated or unsupported** |
| **B — Smallest evaluation unit** | **One claim** of `answer_text` |
| **C — What the judge may see** | The claim and the assembled context in full — **the same judgment used for Faithfulness**, per RO-18 D-8's *"derived from the repository-defined claim-level grounding / faithfulness semantics"* |
| **D — What is authoritative** | The **assembled `Prompt` context** |
| **E — The proposition** | The Faithfulness proposition, read for its negative classes. No second, independent hallucination proposition is introduced — RO-18 D-8: *"No unrelated hallucination formula may be invented"* |
| **F — Positive judgment (a claim counts as hallucinated)** | `NOT_SUPPORTED` — *"not present in the retrieved context at all"* (`glossary.md`), the case `ALTM-INFER-1` describes as *"check assembled prompt for the claim; **if absent, it's fabricated at Infer**"* — **or** `CONTRADICTED`, which is unsupported in the plainest sense and sits below even Groundedness's *"not contradicted"* floor |
| **G — Negative judgment (a claim does not count)** | `SUPPORTED`, **and also `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED`** — a partially supported claim is not fully entailed, so it is not faithful; and it is not *"fabricated"* or *"not present in the context at all"*, so it is not hallucinated |
| **H — Aggregation** | Per case: `hallucinated claims / total claims`. **The denominator is every claim the answer made** — *"Of everything claimed"* — identical to Faithfulness's denominator. Across cases: **macro and micro, both published** |
| **I — Outside the domain** | The `Abstain` outcome (§7.4); whether the context was correct or current; the *"abstention check"* that `ALTM-INFER-2` names **beside** Hallucination Rate — that is a second, separately named check and is **not** one of RO-18's four metrics |

**Why the two are near-complements rather than complements, derived rather than asserted.**

```
Faithfulness      = |{claims : SUPPORTED}|                        / |claims|
Hallucination Rate = |{claims : NOT_SUPPORTED} ∪ {CONTRADICTED}|  / |claims|

Faithfulness + Hallucination Rate  =  1 − |{claims : PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED}| / |claims|
                                   ≤  1,  with equality exactly when no claim is partially supported
```

That is the repository's sentence, exactly: complementary *"in the simple case"*, diverging *"once partial/ambiguous claims exist."* **The divergence is a property of the definitions, not a tolerance.** A judgment vocabulary without a partial class would force the identity the repository denies — which is why the vocabulary in §9 has four verdicts and not two.

**Contradicted vs. unsupported.** Both count in the numerator, because *"fabricated **or** unsupported"* is one bucket in the only definition the repository gives, and no authority distinguishes them arithmetically. **They are recorded distinctly in the per-claim evidence** — RO-18 D-8: *"M2-08 MUST preserve enough evidence to explain a metric result."* A contradiction is a different failure from a fabrication and a reader must be able to see which occurred, even though the rate treats them alike.

### 6.4 Answer Relevancy

| | |
|---|---|
| **A — What is evaluated** | Whether the delivered answer **directly, completely, and only** addresses what was actually asked |
| **B — Smallest evaluation unit** | **The whole answer**, judged against the question. This is a **Final Answer**-stage metric (`altm.md` §4, §6, `ALTM-FINAL-ANSWER-1`), not a per-claim one |
| **C — What the judge may see** | **The question and `answer_text`, and nothing else** |
| **D — What is authoritative** | **The question.** It is user input, not a system output — the only one of the four metrics whose reference is not produced by the system (§8) |
| **E — The proposition** | *Does this answer directly, completely, and only address this question?* — judged as the three conjuncts the definition names, not as one undifferentiated verdict |
| **F — Positive judgment** | All three conjuncts hold: **directly** (it addresses what was asked), **completely** (it addresses all of what was asked), **only** (it introduces no unrequested content) |
| **G — Negative judgment** | Any conjunct fails. The three are reported separately, so *"the second scores lower"* in the reference document's own example — a correct answer padded with unrequested biography — is representable as a failure of *only* alone, without a weighting or a score scale being invented |
| **H — Aggregation** | Per case the metric is the conjunction, so the unit count is one per case and **macro and micro coincide**; that is stated rather than presented as two figures. **The three conjunct rates are published beside it** as evidence |
| **I — Outside the domain** | **Truthfulness, in both directions** — a false answer can be fully relevant and a true answer fully irrelevant (`AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 5: *"independent of whether it's true"*; `altm.md` §6: *"the only metric independent of truthfulness"*). Faithfulness and correctness are excluded by RO-18 D-8 by name. The ***"task-specific rubric"*** `altm.md` §4 names **beside** Answer Relevancy is a second instrument, is not part of this metric, and **is not authorized or defined here**. The `Abstain` outcome (§7.4) |

**Completeness is part of relevance here, and that is a repository statement, not a transplant.** `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 5 says *"directly, **completely**, and only"*, and `glossary.md` repeats *"directly and completely."* The M2-07 correction — that **relevance is not sufficiency** — is binding **for the Retrieve-stage relevance metric it was made about**, and the M2-07 report itself draws the boundary in this direction: *"Whether something 'directly, **completely**, and only address[es] what was actually asked' is **Answer Relevancy**, a **Layer 5 / Final Answer** metric"* — cited there precisely as the standard Context Precision must **not** use. Importing M2-07's standard into Answer Relevancy would erase the distinction M2-07 was written to protect. **The two standards differ because the repository says they differ.**

## 7. Faithfulness vs. Groundedness — the hard gate

### 7.1 The distinction, in repository terms

**Groundedness is Faithfulness with the evidence scope narrowed from the whole context to a single passage.**

| | **Faithfulness** | **Groundedness** |
|---|---|---|
| Evaluation unit | one claim | one claim *(same)* |
| **Evidence scope** | **the assembled context as a whole** | **one specific, citable passage, in isolation** |
| Combining passages | **permitted** — *"even if derived by combining multiple sentences"*, *"satisfied by multi-hop reasoning across sources"* | **not sufficient** — *"weaker on claims that require combining sources without a single direct citation"* |
| Relation named by the repository | — | *"stricter than Faithfulness"* (`altm.md` §6, `glossary.md`); *"stricter than 'not contradicted'"* (`AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md`) |
| Relation in RO-18 D-8's words | **whole-context support** | **per-passage attribution** |
| Judge input signature | (claim, all passages) | (claim, one passage) |

RO-18 Decision 8 names the distinction that must survive in exactly these words: *"The repository distinction between **whole-context support and per-passage attribution** MUST be preserved."* The distinction adopted here **is** that sentence, operationalized.

**Which of the candidate bases it rests on, stated explicitly.** Not evaluation unit (identical — both per claim). Not claim-level vs answer-level (both claim-level). Not contradiction (contradiction fails both, and the repository places Groundedness *above* the not-contradicted floor, not at it). **It rests on the evidence relationship — the scope of evidence admitted for a single claim — which is the *support vs. attribution* distinction RO-18 D-8 names.** Reference semantics do not differ: both reference the assembled context. Only the granularity at which that reference is admitted differs.

### 7.2 The consequences the definitions produce

```
Grounded(claim)  ⟹  Faithful(claim)          a single passage entailing a claim is part of the whole context
Faithful(claim)  ⇏  Grounded(claim)          the multi-hop case
Groundedness  ≤  Faithfulness                per case, and in both aggregations
```

This is what *"stricter than Faithfulness"* means arithmetically, and it is a derived property, not a rule imposed on the judge. **`Grounded ∧ ¬Faithful` is not a representable labelling.** Should a run produce it, that is an **instrument inconsistency to be reported**, not a metric result — the diagnostic precedent M2-07 §9 set when Context Precision 0/5 alongside Context Recall 3/3 revealed a mis-answered proposition rather than a low score.

### 7.3 What the gate forbids

Both metrics collapsing into *"does the answer agree with the context?"* is exactly the failure this gate exists to prevent, and it is detectable: §11 probes **FG-1** and **FG-7** assign **Faithful = yes, Grounded = no** to the same input. **Any implementation returning equal values on those two probes has collapsed the distinction.**

### 7.4 Abstention, across all four metrics

`GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` **G-8**: `statements` is empty **iff** `outcome == "Abstain"`; §9.3: the Abstain `answer_text` *"is a fixed text that asserts nothing about the corpus"*, and the guarantees hold *"vacuously … because nothing is claimed."*

An `Abstain` result therefore **makes no claim**, so all three claim-level metrics have a **zero denominator**, and Answer Relevancy has no assertion to judge against the question.

**The response is a documented domain statement, following `docs/M2.07_Native_Context_Metrics_Report.md` §4.1 exactly: partition and publish both figures, never a filtered number alone.** Report the claim-level metrics and Answer Relevancy over the **20 `Answer` entries** (the domain-faithful figure) with the **all-22** figure published beside them. *"A partition that only ever appeared as a filtered number would be indistinguishable from a partition chosen for its number."* No judge call is made on a zero-claim case — the no-call convention `evaluation/retrieval_metrics.py` documents and M2-07 §3.4 carried into evaluation.

**Answer Relevancy over an abstention is an authority gap, and it is recorded rather than filled** — see §12 finding **U-3**. `ALTM-INFER-2` names an *"abstention check"* beside Hallucination Rate; that check is **not one of RO-18's four metrics and is not defined here.**

## 8. Reference / evidence semantics, and the circularity analysis

### 8.1 Authoritative reference vs. system output, per metric

| Metric | Authoritative reference | Is that reference a system output? | Why it is admissible |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Faithfulness** | assembled `Prompt` context, as a whole | **Yes** — it is the output of Retrieve + Assemble | It is the **input to the stage under test**, not its output. Measuring an output against its own input is the definition of that stage's fidelity |
| **Groundedness** | each passage of the assembled context, individually | **Yes** — same artifact, finer granularity | same |
| **Hallucination Rate** | assembled `Prompt` context, as a whole | **Yes** | same |
| **Answer Relevancy** | **the question** | **No — user input** | Nothing produced by the system participates |

### 8.2 The M2-07 principle, and why it is satisfied rather than violated

The binding principle is that **retrieved context is not automatically authoritative ground truth.** It is satisfied here in the only way the repository allows, and the distinction is worth stating precisely:

- The context is the **reference frame** of the Infer-stage metrics **by definition** — `roadmap.md` §5 Layer 3: *"Checks consistency with retrieved context only"*; `altm.md` §6: these metrics *"say nothing about whether the retrieved evidence was itself correct or current"*; `altm.md` §4 Infer: *"this stage never checks whether the retrieved evidence itself was current or correct — that is a Knowledge-stage concern."*
- It is **not ground truth**. `glossary.md` §Ground Truth vs. Knowledge keeps the two apart, and `roadmap.md` §5 states the consequence in one sentence: ***"A model can be 100% faithful to a stale document."***

**Recorded as a binding reporting constraint: no M2-08 result may be presented as a statement that an answer is correct, or that the corpus is correct or current.** A high Faithfulness figure means the answer is consistent with what it was given, and nothing more.

Why M2-07's situation was the opposite: Context Recall's reference had to be the independently-authored golden `expected_answer` because using retrieval output as the recall denominator would have measured retrieval against itself — output against output. Here the answer (output of Infer) is measured against the context (input to Infer). **Different direction, different property.**

### 8.3 The circularity audit

Every candidate that could silently become an authoritative reference, checked against RO-18 and the Generation Contract:

| Candidate | Admitted? | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The system's **retrieved context** | **Yes, as the defined reference frame only** — never as ground truth | §8.2 |
| The system's **generated answer** | **Never as a reference.** It is the object under evaluation. No proposition takes it as evidence | The three claim-level propositions take the context as evidence; P1 decomposes the answer and asserts nothing about it |
| The system's **`SupportingEvidence`** — its own attribution | **Never.** This is the trap `GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §24.3 exists to prevent | §3.1. The contract guarantees the evidence's **provenance**, not that the claim is entailed by it. Groundedness re-establishes the attribution empirically, against each passage independently, and never reads what the system claimed to cite |
| The system's **`statements[]`** as the claim set | **Never as the metric's unit.** Diagnostic evidence only | §5.2 — and it would let the denominator be set by the component under test |
| A **previous evaluation result** or a **prior judge output** | **Never.** Each judgment is a function of its own inputs; no proposition receives another proposition's output as evidence | The proposition inputs in §9 are exhaustive |
| **Another derived metric** | **Never.** Hallucination Rate reads the *same judged verdicts* as Faithfulness (RO-18 D-8's *"derived from the repository-defined claim-level … semantics"*) — it does not read Faithfulness's *value* | §6.3. Reading the value would produce `1 − F`, which the repository denies |
| The golden **`expected_answer`** | **Not used by any of the four.** Correctness against ground truth is not among RO-18's four metrics | Faithfulness/Groundedness/Hallucination reference the context (`roadmap.md` §5 Layer 3); Answer Relevancy is *"independent of whether it's true"*. Showing the reference answer to the relevancy judge would convert relevance into correctness — the collapse RO-18 D-8 forbids by name |

**The golden dataset is still read — for the questions, and for `expected_outcome` to partition the abstention domain (§7.4).** Supplying a case is not being that case's reference; M2-07 §4.1 set the same precedent.

**Structural enforceability.** Each exclusion above is expressible as an absent parameter rather than a promise about a call site — the property M2-07 established with `decomposition_prompt(question, reference_answer)`, which *"has no parameter through which retrieved context could arrive."* The propositions in §9 are stated with their inputs closed for the same reason. **Prescribing how a later sprint realizes that is outside this artifact's scope; the requirement that it be structural is not.**

## 9. Judge propositions

Conceptual only. **No SDK, adapter, client, retry policy, router, orchestration, HTTP concern, prompt wording, module, signature or dependency is designed here** — RO-18 Decision 4 fixes the seam shape (*native M2-08 evaluator → injected judge seam → existing provider boundary*) and the later implementation sprint owns the mechanics.

Each proposition is a **closed question fixed before any metric is computed**, never a score the judge produces directly — the discipline `docs/M2.07_Native_Context_Metrics_Report.md` §3.3 records.

| # | Proposition | Inputs | **Explicitly excluded from the judge** | Judgment |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **P1** | *What individual claims does this answer make?* | the question; `answer_text` | the assembled context; any individual passage; `statements[]`; `supporting_evidence`; the golden `expected_answer`; any prior verdict | one claim per line |
| **P2** | *Is this claim supported by the context taken as a whole?* | one claim; the **whole** assembled context | the question; the golden `expected_answer`; the system's attribution; any prior verdict | one of `SUPPORTED` / `PARTIALLY_SUPPORTED` / `CONTRADICTED` / `NOT_SUPPORTED` |
| **P3** | *Is this claim supported by this single passage, on its own?* | one claim; **exactly one** passage | every other passage; the question; the golden `expected_answer`; the system's attribution | `SUPPORTED` / `NOT_SUPPORTED` |
| **P4** | *Does this answer directly, completely, and only address this question?* | the question; `answer_text` | the assembled context; any passage; the golden `expected_answer`; any claim verdict | three verdicts: `DIRECT`/`NOT_DIRECT`, `COMPLETE`/`NOT_COMPLETE`, `ONLY`/`NOT_ONLY` |

**Multiplicity.** P1 is judged **once per case**. P2 is judged **once per claim**. P3 is judged **once per (claim, passage) pair** — a claim is grounded if any one of its passage judgments is `SUPPORTED`. P4 is judged **once per case**. Faithfulness, Groundedness and Hallucination Rate each require **more than one judgment per case**; Answer Relevancy requires **one**.

**Why P1 sees the question but not the context.** It sees the question because an answer's claims are frequently elliptical — *"Around 7.5 years"* is a claim only once the question supplies its subject; `decomposition_prompt(question, reference_answer)` set that precedent. It must **not** see the context, because a decomposer that can see what the context supports may suppress claims the context does not — deflating the denominator and inflating Faithfulness in the system's favour. **The denominator must not be a function of the evidence it will be scored against.**

**Why P2 and P3 do not see the question.** Support is a relation between a claim and a text. Admitting the question invites drift toward relevance — the proposition-drift failure M2-07 §9 caught and corrected.

**Why P4 sees neither the context nor the reference answer.** Both would let the judge grade truth, which `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 5 and `ALTM-FINAL-ANSWER-1` (*"not against truthfulness metrics"*) exclude, and RO-18 D-8 forbids by name.

**Invalid or ambiguous judgment.** A response outside a proposition's declared vocabulary is an **error to be surfaced**, never silently coerced to a default or to the favourable class — the convention `evaluation/context_metrics.py`'s `parse_verdict(answer, vocabulary, proposition)` already establishes. **Whether a later sprint retries, reports, or aborts is its decision; that an out-of-vocabulary verdict may not be counted as a metric input is fixed here.**

**Determinism boundary, stated in advance.** **Everything except the judge's answers is deterministic.** The judge's answers are not, and **the claim denominator is itself a judge product (P1)** — a wider non-determinism than a fixed-denominator metric carries. `GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` **G-9** under v2.0.0 already withholds reproducibility for `answer_text`, so the *object* being measured is non-reproducible too. **No M2-08 figure may be presented as deterministic.** RO-18 Decision 6 and RO-14 Decision 2 remain binding: **no live judge call may enter the deterministic pytest suite.**

**U-2 — Groundedness's claim domain: "every individual claim" vs. "every important/substantive claim."**
`AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` (*"every **individual** claim"*) and `altm.md` §6 (*"**each** claim"*) carry no importance qualifier; `glossary.md` (*"every **important** claim"*) and RO-18 D-8 (*"**substantive** answer claims"*) do. **Reported, not silently reconciled.** No repository source establishes a hierarchy between them, and none defines an importance standard. The reading adopted in §6.2 is the only one that requires no invention — every claim P1 yields is in domain, with `GENERATION_CONTRACT.md` §9.3's *"asserts nothing about the corpus"* as the repository's own negative form. **If the Repository Owner intends a stricter importance filter, that filter needs a definition the repository does not currently contain**, and supplying one would be inventing metric semantics, which RO-18 D-8 bars.

**U-3 — Answer Relevancy is undefined over an `Abstain` outcome.**
No authority states whether a conforming abstention *"directly, completely, and only"* addresses the question it declines. Both available answers are unsupported: scoring 0 penalizes the behaviour `expected_outcome == "Abstain"` requires; scoring 1 invents a rule. **Handled by the M2-07 §4.1 precedent — partition and publish both figures — not by a definition.** `ALTM-INFER-2`'s *"abstention check"* is named beside Hallucination Rate as a **second instrument**; it is not one of RO-18's four metrics and **no abstention check is defined, designed or authorized here.**

**U-4 — `Hallucination Rate` is absent from the golden dataset's `expected_metrics`.**
All 22 entries of `datasets/golden/resume_evidence_trace.json` carry the identical list *Faithfulness, Groundedness, Context Recall, Context Precision, Answer Relevancy* — **Hallucination Rate appears in none.** It is nonetheless one of RO-18's four, and `altm.md` §5/§6 and `ALTM-INFER-1`/`ALTM-INFER-2` place it at Infer. **The dataset was not modified**, and the field is a per-question expectation annotation, not a metric definition. Recorded so that a later sprint does not read the omission as a scope statement in either direction.

**U-5 — the "Evaluated by" mechanism note attached to Answer Relevancy is a tool attribution, not a definition.**
`AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` §Layer 5 reads *"Evaluated by: DeepEval (embedding-similarity between reverse-engineered questions and the original query)."* **That parenthetical is not adopted, for three independent reasons.** It sits in the *Evaluated by* line, not the *Definition* column, and RO-18 D-8 bars adopting *"generic framework terminology … as a definition."* RO-18 D-2 removes DeepEval from M2-08's path. And RO-18 D-7 closes the embedding boundary explicitly — *"`BGEEmbeddingProvider` is not authorized for evaluation merely because it exists in the repository for retrieval"* — so the mechanism could not be implemented without a dependency the ruling refuses. **The same disposition applies to the `Evaluated by: DeepEval` and `Evaluated by: Ragas` lines generally: they are tool attributions, and RO-18 §4.10 declines to amend the Tool column while rescoping the mechanism.** Nothing is amended here.

**U-6 — Answer Relevancy's layer placement differs between two documents, and its ALTM stage does not.**
`roadmap.md` §5 groups all four metrics under **Layer 3 — Generation Quality**; `AI_Quality_Metrics_Reference.md` places Faithfulness/Groundedness/Hallucination Rate at **Layer 4 — Generation Quality** and Answer Relevancy alone at **Layer 5 — Task Quality**. RO-18 §4.10 already dispositions the numbering: *"Both numberings are correct in their own document and neither is amended here."* **The ALTM stage attribution is consistent across every source** — `altm.md` §4, §6 and `ALTM-FINAL-ANSWER-1` all place Answer Relevancy at **Final Answer**, not Infer — and `roadmap.md` §5 states its own grouping is by tool, since its layers correspond *"to specific ALTM stages, not to a specific tool's marketing scope."* **This is operationally load-bearing and is why Answer Relevancy alone is judged without the context (§6.4).** Recorded; nothing amended.
